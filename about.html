About TyreXpress

About the Founder

TyreXpress was founded by Sipho Mthembu, a seasoned mechanic with over 15 years of experience in the automotive industry. Born and raised in Soweto, Sipho started the business with a simple goal: to offer honest, reliable tyre services to everyday South Africans. His passion for road safety and community upliftment drives everything we do.

Our Mission

We believe that tyre care shouldn’t be complicated or expensive. Our mission is to provide fast, friendly service with transparent pricing, so you can drive with confidence and peace of mind.

Community Involvement

TyreXpress is more than just a tyre shop — we’re part of the community. We sponsor local soccer teams, support youth driving safety workshops, and offer free tyre checks during festive seasons to help reduce road accidents. We’re proud to give back where we can.

Our Services

We offer a full range of tyre services including puncture repairs, wheel balancing, alignment, and tyre replacements. We stock trusted brands like Dunlop, Goodyear, and Continental, and we’re always happy to help you choose the right fit for your car and budget.
